# HC-quintic-sextic

Code and data supporting the note *The Hodge conjecture for very general sums of binary quintics and sextics* (Kevin
Saller, 2026), DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23267886](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23267886).

**Result.** For d = 5 or 6 and every m ≥ 1, the Hodge conjecture holds for the hypersurface
F_0(u_0,v_0) + … + F_m(u_m,v_m) = 0 in P^(2m+1) when F_0, …, F_m are very general binary forms of degree d. The note
also gives the dimension of the space of Hodge classes. It further proves the Hodge conjecture for some special
members: for d ≥ 5, d ≠ 6, when some of the forms are fixed, the Hodge conjecture holds for the hypersurface defined
by them, and the other forms are very general relative to them; and for d = 6 when one form is arbitrary and the others
are very general relative to it. For d ≥ 5, d ≠ 6 and very general forms it shows, using a theorem of Degtyarev and
Shimada, that the integral Hodge classes are generated by classes of linear subspaces, so the integral Hodge
conjecture holds. Finally, for d ≥ 5 the Hodge conjecture holds for products of such hypersurfaces with independent
very general forms.

The proofs in the note do not use these computations. [`README.txt`](README.txt) gives the requirements, the rerun
instructions and what each script checks.

**Status:** not yet peer reviewed.

**Disclosure:** the arguments and computations were produced with the assistance of an AI system (Claude, by
Anthropic). They were checked with separately written code and in separate internal reviews, which were also carried
out with AI assistance. What was not checked is listed in the note and in README.txt.
